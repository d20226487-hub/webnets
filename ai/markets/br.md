### Market context

- Market: Brazil (`br`), languages pt, currency BRL
- Minimum age: 18+
- Products allowed: sports, casino
- Promotions allowed: none; restricted: welcome_bonus, deposit_bonus, free_bets, promo_code
- Payment methods: pix, ted, debit
- App install paths: iOS app-store, Android both
- Notes: Regulated under Lei 14.790/2023. Licensed operators use .bet.br domains. Advance bonuses are prohibited; PIX is the default payment rail; credit cards are not accepted.

### Mandated texts (from the market settings)

- Age notice: Proibido para menores de 18 anos.
- Self-exclusion: Você pode solicitar a autoexclusão a qualquer momento.

### Rules you must not break

- Every page opens with an age gate (18+) before any gambling content.
- Every page has the responsible-gambling panel (usually in the footer) with the market text: age notice, licence line, taglines, self-exclusion and helpline where given. Never type this text yourself; it comes from the market settings.
- Only licensed operators with .bet.br domains; links go to the .bet.br domain.
- Payments are PIX, TED and debit cards; never show credit cards.
- Identity and age are verified with CPF before play.
