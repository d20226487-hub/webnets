### Market context

- Market: Kazakhstan (`kz`), languages ru, kk, currency KZT
- Minimum age: 21+
- Products allowed: sports; not allowed: casino
- Promotions allowed: none; restricted: welcome_bonus, deposit_bonus, free_bets, promo_code
- Payment methods: kaspi, visa, mastercard, halyk
- App install paths: iOS app-store, Android apk
- Notes: Online bets are processed through the Unified Accounting Centre (ЕУЦ). Online casino is not permitted: casino-vertical blocks are disabled for this market.

### Rules you must not break

- Every page opens with an age gate (21+) before any gambling content.
- Every page has the responsible-gambling panel (usually in the footer) with the market text: age notice, licence line, taglines, self-exclusion and helpline where given. Never type this text yourself; it comes from the market settings.
- Minimum age is 21: the age gate and every age line say 21+.
- No online casino content anywhere; only sports betting.
- Identification goes through eGov (or video identification); say so on sign-up pages.
- Self-limitation is available through eGov; mention it on responsible-gambling content.
